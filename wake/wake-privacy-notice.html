This Privacy Policy explains how the Wake app for Jira Cloud accesses, processes, stores, and logs End-User Data. It supplements Totesoft's general privacy policy at https://www.totesoft.com/privacy-policy. To the extent that any provision of the General Policy conflicts with the provisions of this App-Specific Policy regarding Wake, the provisions of this App-Specific Policy shall prevail.

1. Scope and roles

Wake is an Atlassian Forge dashboard gadget. A user selects a saved Jira filter and display fields. Wake retrieves matching issues, displays a table, groups issues, calculates counts or numeric aggregates, and creates charts. Licensed functionality may add nested grouping, additional metrics, expanded paging, and CSV export.
  1. Licensed Authorized Jira User (Customer) - Configures the Wake gadget on a dashboard by selecting a saved Jira filter, the fields to display, the fields to group by, and any numeric aggregation to apply. Wake reads issues and presents them; it holds no Jira write permission and does not modify issue data.
  2. The Customer is the data controller of all End-User Data processed by the app.
    1. Totesoft personnel have no routine access to End-User Data. The narrow exception is the diagnostic application logging described in section 5, which is accessible through Atlassian's Forge tooling to authorized personnel with a support, security, or operational need. As set out in section 5, those logs carry operational signals only — not issue field values, saved-filter query text, or Jira response content. Wake does not store or transfer End-User Data outside the Atlassian platform.
    2. The customer controls which gadgets and filters are configured and remains responsible for the Jira content placed in those gadgets and for providing required notices to its users.
    3. The End-User who likes to view the gadget must have the necessary Jira permissions to view the data.

The customer controls the saved filters, Jira permissions, dashboard access, selected fields, and content stored in Jira.

2. Data Wake accesses and processes

Depending on configuration and Jira permissions, Wake may access or process:

  1. Jira site, user, dashboard, gadget, installation, and license context supplied by Atlassian;
  2. Saved-filter identifiers, names, JQL, descriptions, owners, and share permissions;
  3. Field metadata, including field identifiers, names, and schema information;
  4. Issue identifiers and issue keys;
  5. Configured display and grouping fields;
  6. Standard fields required for display, currently including summary, issue type, status, priority, and subtask information;
  7. Numeric and categorical field values selected by the user;
  8. User-related Jira fields selected for display or grouping, which may contain names, account identifiers, avatars, or other personal data supplied by Jira;
  9. Counts, sums, averages, grouped results, charts, and other values derived from the issues loaded; and
  10. Technical status, retry, pagination, rate-limit, and error information.

Wake intentionally avoids requesting all Jira fields and is designed to exclude heavy fields such as descriptions, attachments, comments, and worklogs from selectable/display fetches. The customer remains responsible for choosing fields and filters appropriate for dashboard viewers.

3. Data Export (CSV)

Wake is read-only with respect to Jira issues. It does not intentionally create, edit, transition, or delete issues.

When a licensed feature allows CSV export, the export is generated from the issues already loaded and downloaded through the user's browser. Wake does not send the CSV to a Totesoft server. Once downloaded, the file is controlled by the user and the customer's device, browser, storage, security, and retention policies.

4. Configuration and Forge hosted storage

Jira dashboard gadget configuration is submitted to and maintained through Atlassian's gadget/Forge configuration mechanisms, which resides within your own Atlassian site.

Issue data is not stored — it is fetched live each time the gadget renders.

5. Application logs and Totesoft access

Wake writes diagnostic events to Atlassian's Forge application logs. These logs are hosted on Atlassian's developer platform and are accessible to authorized Totesoft personnel who hold access to the app's developer environments. Access is limited to personnel with a support, security, or operational need.

Diagnostic entries are limited to non-content operational signals: event names, saved-filter and gadget identifiers, field identifiers, license state, HTTP status codes, retry and pagination state, result counts, and opaque correlation values. Wake does not write issue field values, saved-filter query text, or Jira response content to its application logs.

Where a diagnostic entry needs to distinguish one query from another — for example to confirm that a paginated request series ran against a single unchanged query — Wake records a non-reversible fingerprint of the query rather than the query text.

  1. Totesoft does not operate a separate remote application server for Wake.
  2. Wake makes no network calls to non-Atlassian hosts and uses no third-party analytics or error-reporting service.

What the app does not do

  • No data leaves the Atlassian platform: the app makes no network calls to any non-Atlassian host.
  • No third-party subprocessors are used beyond Atlassian's own Forge platform.
  • No credentials, tokens, or secrets are collected or stored.

What the app writes

The app does not modify issues. It writes only its own gadget configuration.

Retention and deletion

Gadget configuration persists while the gadget exists; removing the gadget from a dashboard stops its use. If the app is uninstalled, data in Atlassian Forge hosted storage is soft-deleted by Atlassian: reinstalling the app within 21 days relinks it to the existing data, and after that window the data is permanently deleted in accordance with Atlassian's standard data retention and disposal policy. Contact us to request earlier removal of stored configuration.

Data subject requests

For access or deletion requests relating to data handled by the app, contact info@totesoft.com. Because all app data resides in your own Atlassian site, your Jira administrators can also inspect or remove it directly.

Changes

We will update this notice when the app's data handling changes and revise the date above. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
