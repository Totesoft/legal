## Support scope

Totesoft supports installation, license-state behavior, gadget configuration, saved-filter access, field selection, grouping, metrics, charts, CSV export, rate-limit behavior, partial-result warnings, and confirmed defects in supported Wake versions.

Target initial response: {{SUPPORT_RESPONSE_TARGET}}. This is a service target, not a guaranteed SLA unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

## Information to include

- Jira Cloud site URL or site identifier;
- Wake version and environment, when known;
- active, trial, or inactive license state;
- saved-filter identifier, but not the full JQL unless specifically requested through an approved secure channel;
- number of matching and loaded issues shown by the gadget;
- selected field identifiers and grouping/metric configuration;
- browser and approximate time of failure;
- steps to reproduce; and
- sanitized screenshots or logs.

Do not send passwords, API tokens, full issue exports, confidential JQL, or unnecessary personal information. Redact issue summaries, user names, customer names, and numeric business values unless support specifically requires them and the customer authorizes disclosure.

## Security reports

Report suspected security vulnerabilities to [{{SECURITY_CONTACT_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{SECURITY_CONTACT_EMAIL}}). Include a concise description, affected version, reproduction steps, and impact. Do not publicly disclose an unresolved vulnerability or include live customer data in the initial report.
